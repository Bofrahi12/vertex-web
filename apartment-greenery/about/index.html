About Apartment Greenery

Hi, and welcome. Apartment Greenery is an independent blog about one very specific thing: growing healthy indoor plants in apartments -- the kind with north-facing windows, radiators under the sills, curious cats, and approximately one good spot for plants that three different plants are already fighting over.

Why This Blog Exists

Most plant advice on the internet assumes you have a sunroom. Apartment dwellers don't. This blog is written for real apartment conditions: limited light, dry heated air, small pots, and landlords who notice holes in walls.

How Our Guides Are Written

Every guide here is researched from trusted horticultural sources and written to be genuinely useful -- no copied care tags, no "just add water!" hand-waving. Where plant care involves judgment calls (like watering frequency), we explain the reasoning so you can adapt it to your own home.

We're plant enthusiasts writing for fellow apartment gardeners -- not botanists, and we don't pretend to be. For serious plant diseases or valuable specimens, a local nursery or cooperative extension service is the right call.

What We Cover

  • Plant care guides -- specific, practical how-tos for popular houseplants
  • Troubleshooting -- yellow leaves, pests, legginess, and other mysteries, diagnosed honestly
  • Small-space setups -- shelves, lighting, and layouts that work in rentals
  • Product-type guidance -- what to look for in grow lights, soil, and pots, without hype

What We Don't Do

  • We don't publish thin or filler content.
  • We don't make medical or health claims about plants.
  • We don't hype products -- our buying guides explain what to look for, not which brand to buy.

A Note on Advertising

To keep the site running, we plan to display ads through Google AdSense in the future. Advertising will never influence what we write -- if a product type is bad, we'll say so. See our Privacy Policy for details on cookies and advertising.

Thanks for stopping by. If a guide here saves one of your plants, we've done our job.
