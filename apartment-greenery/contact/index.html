Before You Write

A few things that help us help you faster:

  • Which plant is it, and what specifically is happening?
  • Where does it sit -- which direction does the window face, and how far is the plant from it?
  • How do you water -- how often, and do you check the soil first?

A photo of the plant (and its spot in the room) is worth a thousand words.
