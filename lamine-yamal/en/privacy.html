Privacy policy

A fan-made site

This is an unofficial fan tribute site — not an official site of Lamine Yamal, FC Barcelona, or any football federation. All names and trademarks belong to their owners.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data at all: no accounts, no forms, no sign-ups. The pages are static and delivered to you as they are.

Storage on your device

The poll and quiz save your choices in your browser only (localStorage) so you do not vote twice, and the site caches its files on your device to open faster (Service Worker). You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Third parties

Videos are embedded from YouTube via youtube-nocookie; when you play a video, YouTube (Google) may use its own cookies — see the Google privacy policy. WhatsApp links open the WhatsApp app directly.

Contact

For any privacy or advertising question, contact us on WhatsApp via the "Book your ad" button on the site.
