Ballon d'Or night: Yamal makes history again

🏆

Official tonight: Kopa Trophy 2026 — the third in a row!

At the Ballon d'Or ceremony in London tonight, Lamine Yamal was crowned with the Kopa Trophy for the best young player in the world for the third consecutive year (2024, 2025, 2026) — the first player in history to win it three times in a row. He finished ahead of his teammate Pau Cubarsí and the other nominees, taking his tally to three Kopa Trophies before he even turns 20. (Sources: beIN Sports and Le360 — live coverage of the ceremony)

⏳

The big Ballon d'Or: the result is announced at the same ceremony

Officially: Yamal is in the top three for the 2026 Ballon d'Or alongside Harry Kane and Kylian Mbappé, after an exceptional season: world champion with Spain, La Liga and Spanish Super Cup winner with Barcelona, and 25 goals and 20 assists in 57 matches. Note: the award's official website mistakenly published pre-written "winner" articles for all three finalists before the ceremony and they were taken down — the only result that counts is the one officially announced at the ceremony, and this page will be updated as soon as it is confirmed.

The full story

How did he become a star? Six chapters

He didn't get here by luck. He got here through a family that crossed the sea, a working-class neighbourhood that taught him never to give up, and a talent that can't be taught.

CHAPTER 01

The roots: from Larache… to Rocafonda

The story begins before Lamine was even born. His grandmother Fatima took a bus alone from Morocco to Mataró, and worked three shifts a day so she could bring over her son Mounir when he was three years old. His father, Mounir Nasraoui, is from Larache in Morocco, and his mother, Sheila Ebana, is from Bata in Equatorial Guinea.

Lamine was born on 13 July 2007 in Esplugues de Llobregat, and grew up in Rocafonda, the working-class neighbourhood of Mataró. That's where he learned his football on the street — and where his character was forged.

304His famous finger celebration is the last three digits of his neighbourhood's postcode (08304) — so the whole world never forgets where he came from.
CHAPTER 02
2007

The chosen child: Messi himself bathed the baby

In 2007, at a UNICEF charity photo shoot, a photograph was taken that would become legendary: a young Lionel Messi bathing a baby of a few months in a small tub… that baby was Lamine Yamal. The photo resurfaced at Euro 2024 and the world couldn't believe its eyes.

In 2014, aged seven, he moved from local club La Torreta to La Masia, Barcelona's academy. The coaches quickly realised this boy was like no one else: he dribbled without fear, and saw the pass before the ball even arrived.

La MasiaHe rose through every age group, always playing with boys a year or two older — and the difference never showed.
CHAPTER 03

The explosion: the youngest player in Barcelona history

29 April 2023: Xavi brings him on against Real Betis, aged 15 years, 9 months and 16 days — the youngest player to wear the Barcelona shirt in an official match in more than a century of the club's history.

In October 2023 he scored his first goal, against Granada, and became the youngest scorer in La Liga history. The press started writing his name next to the names of legends — while he was still at school.

15 years oldThe age at which he started with the first team — before he could even drive or vote.
CHAPTER 04

King of Europe: Euro 2024

In Germany, he became the youngest player ever to appear at a European Championship, and then its youngest scorer, with a fantasy goal against France in the semi-final: a curling shot from outside the box that UEFA voted the goal of the tournament.

In the final against England, he set up the opening goal and lifted the trophy aged 17 years and one day, along with the tournament's Best Young Player award. Before the tournament, Rabiot had said of him "he'll have to do more than that"… the answer came on the pitch.

16 years, 362 daysHis age the day he scored against France — a record you can't even get close to unless you're exceptional.
CHAPTER 05

The number 10: heir to the throne at Barcelona

The 2024/25 season: a domestic treble with Barcelona — La Liga, the Copa del Rey and the Spanish Super Cup. He won the Kopa Trophy in 2024 and 2025, becoming the first player in history to win it twice in a row, and finished runner-up in the 2025 Ballon d'Or behind Dembélé, aged 18.

On 16 July 2025, he was officially handed the number 10 shirt — the number of Messi, Ronaldinho and Maradona — and signed a contract until 2031 with a release clause of €1 billion. The club said it out loud: this is the future.

€1 billionThe value of the release clause in his contract — Barcelona slammed the door shut on the whole world.
CHAPTER 06

World champion: and the story has only just begun

The 2025/26 season: Barcelona's top scorer with 24 goals and 17 assists, La Liga's Player of the Season, and the first hat-trick of his career, against Villarreal in February 2026.

And in the summer of 2026, he lifted the World Cup with Spain after a final against Argentina, aged 19. From the Rocafonda neighbourhood to the top of the world in three years of professional football… and everyone says: the best is yet to come.

19 years oldEuropean champion and world champion, Ballon d'Or runner-up, and Barcelona's number 10 — before turning 20.

Playing style — the star's weapons

Defenders know exactly what he's going to do… and they still can't stop him.

🌀

One-on-one dribbling

He receives on the touchline, squares up his defender, and with one touch of his left foot changes direction. Among the most successful dribblers in Europe — as a teenager.

🎯

The curling shot

His signature: he cuts in from the right and bends it into the far corner — just like his goal against France at Euro 2024, and against Inter in the Champions League semi-final.

👁️

Vision and the trivela

He sees the pass before others even think of it, and plays outside-of-the-boot balls (the trivela) with his left that split entire defences — his playmaking is on the same level as his scoring.

🧊

Ice in his veins

Finals, El Clásico, a European semi-final… the bigger the moment, the bigger he gets. Rabiot challenged him before the Euro semi-final — he answered with the goal of the tournament.

Visitors' questions

The questions people ask about Yamal

Who is Lamine Yamal?

Lamine Yamal Nasraoui Ebana (born 13 July 2007) is a Spanish right winger who plays for FC Barcelona and the Spain national team. A La Masia graduate, the youngest player in Barcelona's history, Euro 2024 and 2026 World Cup champion, and winner of the Kopa Trophy three years in a row — he is widely regarded as the finest talent of his generation.

Is Lamine Yamal Moroccan?

He is Spanish by birth and by national team, but his roots on his father's side are Moroccan: Mounir Nasraoui is from the city of Larache, and his grandmother Fatima is the one who emigrated from Morocco to Catalonia and worked hard to bring her family over. His mother, Sheila Ebana, is from Bata in Equatorial Guinea. Yamal is proud of his roots and celebrates with his neighbourhood's "304" after every goal.

How old is he, and when did he start?

He was born on 13 July 2007, so he is 19 years old in 2026. He started with Barcelona's first team in April 2023, aged 15 years, 9 months and 16 days — a record in the club's history.

What is his shirt number, and what about his contract?

He has worn the number 10 for Barcelona since July 2025 (Messi's historic number), and the number 19 for Spain. His contract with Barcelona runs until June 2031, with a release clause of €1 billion.

Has he won the Ballon d'Or?

He has won the Kopa Trophy (the best young player in the world) three years in a row: 2024, 2025 and 2026 — a historic record. As for the main Ballon d'Or: he finished second in 2025 behind Ousmane Dembélé, and was one of the three finalists at the 2026 ceremony in London.

What is the story of the Messi photo with the baby?

In 2007, a young Lionel Messi took part in a UNICEF charity photo shoot, and as it happened, the baby he was bathing in the famous photo was Lamine Yamal, just a few months old. The photo came back and went viral at Euro 2024, when Yamal had become a Barcelona star — the fans called it "a prophecy in a photograph".

Where can I follow him?

His official Instagram account is @lamineyamal and he has a TikTok account, @lamine.yamal. Beware of fake accounts impersonating him.
