The full story

How did he become a star? Six chapters

He did not get there by luck. He got there through the story of a family that crossed the sea, a working-class neighbourhood that taught him never to give up, and a talent that cannot be taught.

Chapter 01

The roots: from Larache… to Rocafonda

The story begins before Lamine was born. His grandmother Fatima boarded a bus alone from Morocco to Mataró and worked three shifts a day to bring over her son Mounir when he was three years old. His father Mounir Nasraoui is from Larache in Morocco, and his mother Sheila Ebana from Bata in Equatorial Guinea.

Lamine was born on 13 July 2007 in Esplugues de Llobregat and grew up in Rocafonda, the working-class neighbourhood of Mataró. That is where he learned football in the street — and where his character was made.

304His famous finger celebration is the last three digits of his neighbourhood's postcode (08304) — so the whole world never forgets where he came from.
Chapter 02
2007

The promised child: Messi himself bathed the baby

In 2007, at a UNICEF charity photoshoot, a now-legendary picture was taken: a young Lionel Messi bathing a baby of a few months in a small tub… that baby was Lamine Yamal. The photo resurfaced at Euro 2024 and the world could not believe its eyes.

In 2014, aged seven, he moved from La Torreta to La Masia, Barcelona's academy. The coaches quickly noticed this boy was like no other: he dribbled without fear and saw the pass before the ball arrived.

La MasiaHe rose through every age group, always playing with boys a year or two older — and the difference never showed.
Chapter 03

The explosion: youngest player in Barcelona's history

29 April 2023: Xavi brings him on against Real Betis, aged 15 years, 9 months and 16 days — the youngest player ever to wear the Barcelona shirt officially in more than a century of the club's history.

In October 2023 he scored his first goal, against Granada, becoming the youngest scorer in La Liga history. The press began writing his name next to the legends' — while he was still at school.

15 years oldThe age at which he started with the first team — before he could even drive or vote.
Chapter 04

King of Europe: Euro 2024

In Germany he became the youngest player ever to appear at a EURO, and then its youngest scorer with a fantasy goal against France in the semi-final: a curled shot from outside the box that UEFA chose as the Goal of the Tournament.

In the final against England he set up the opening goal and lifted the trophy aged 17 years and one day, with the tournament's Young Player award. Before the tournament, Rabiot had said of him "he'll have to do more than that"… the answer came on the pitch.

16 years 362 daysHis age the day he scored against France — a record you cannot even approach without being exceptional.
Chapter 05

The number 10: heir to the throne at Barcelona

The 2024/25 season: a domestic treble with Barcelona — La Liga, the Copa del Rey and the Super Cup. He won the Kopa Trophy in 2024 and 2025, becoming the first player in history to win it twice in a row, and finished second in the 2025 Ballon d'Or behind Dembélé at just 18.

On 16 July 2025 he officially received the number 10 shirt — the number of Messi, Ronaldinho and Maradona — and signed a contract until 2031 with a release clause of €1 billion. The club said it out loud: this is the future.

€1 billionThe value of his release clause — Barcelona closed the door on the entire world.
Chapter 06

World champion: and the story has only just begun

The 2025/26 season: Barcelona's top scorer with 24 goals and 17 assists, La Liga's Player of the Season, and the first hat-trick of his career, against Villarreal in February 2026.

And in the summer of 2026 he lifted the World Cup with Spain after a final against Argentina, aged 19. From the Rocafonda neighbourhood to the top of the world in three years of professional football… and everyone says: the best is yet to come.

19 years oldEuropean champion and world champion, Ballon d'Or runner-up, and Barcelona's number 10 — before turning 20.

Why is he so dangerous?

Playing style — the star's weapons

Defenders know exactly what he is going to do… and they still cannot stop him.

🌀

One-on-one dribbling

He receives on the touchline, faces up his defender, and with a single touch of his left foot changes direction. One of the most successful dribblers in Europe — as a teenager.

🎯

The curled finish

His trademark: he cuts in from the right and bends it into the far corner — just like his goal against France at Euro 2024 and against Inter in the Champions League semi-final.

👁️

Vision and the trivela

He sees the pass before anyone else even thinks of it, and plays outside-of-the-boot (trivela) balls with his left that open up entire defences — for him, creating is just like scoring.

🧊

Ice-cold nerves

Finals, Clásicos, European semi-finals… the bigger the moment, the bigger he becomes. Rabiot challenged him before the EURO semi-final; he answered with the Goal of the Tournament.

His Moroccanness

A Moroccan heart… from Larache

🇲🇦 The roots he is proud of

Yamal is Spanish by birth and by national team, but his blood is Moroccan on his father's side: his father Mounir Nasraoui is from the city of Larache in northern Morocco, and his grandmother Fatima is the first heroine of the story — she travelled alone by bus from Morocco to Mataró and worked three shifts a day to bring her family together. Even the name "Nasraoui" bears witness to the origin.

Morocco officially tried to recruit him: the Royal Moroccan Football Federation contacted him and his family about playing for the Atlas Lions, and its president Fouzi Lekjaa publicly confirmed the approach — Yamal chose Spain, and Morocco respected his decision. It never diminished Moroccan fans' love for him: his Barcelona matches are followed in Morocco with the passion of national-team games.

And on Ballon d'Or night 2026, the Moroccan presence was double: Yamal winning his third Kopa Trophy, and Moroccan star Ayyoub Bouaddi sixth in the same ranking — a new generation raising Morocco's name on the biggest stages, each in his own way.

Visitors' questions

The questions everyone asks about Yamal

Who is Lamine Yamal?

Lamine Yamal Nasraoui Ebana (born 13 July 2007) is a Spanish right winger who plays for FC Barcelona and the Spain national team. A La Masia graduate, the youngest player in Barcelona's history, Euro 2024 and 2026 World Cup champion, and winner of the Kopa Trophy three times in a row — widely considered the finest talent of his generation.

Is Lamine Yamal Moroccan?

He is Spanish by birth and national team, but his roots are Moroccan on his father's side: Mounir Nasraoui comes from the city of Larache, and his grandmother Fatima was the one who emigrated from Morocco to Catalonia and worked to bring her family over. His mother Sheila Ebana is from Bata in Equatorial Guinea. Yamal is proud of his roots and celebrates with his neighbourhood's number "304" after every goal.

How old is he, and when did he start?

He was born on 13 July 2007, so he is 19 years old in 2026. He debuted for Barcelona's first team in April 2023 aged 15 years, 9 months and 16 days — a record in the club's history.

What is his number and his contract?

He wears the number 10 for Barcelona since July 2025 (Messi's historic number), and the number 19 for Spain. His Barcelona contract runs until June 2031, with a €1 billion release clause.

Has he won the Ballon d'Or?

He has won the Kopa Trophy (best young player in the world) three times in a row: 2024, 2025 and 2026 — a historic record. In the senior Ballon d'Or, he finished second in 2025 behind Ousmane Dembélé, and third in 2026 behind Harry Kane and Kylian Mbappé.

What is the story of the Messi baby photo?

In 2007, a young Lionel Messi took part in a UNICEF charity photoshoot, and the baby he happened to be bathing in the famous picture was Lamine Yamal, just a few months old. The photo resurfaced at Euro 2024, once Yamal had become a Barcelona star — fans called it "a prophecy in a picture".

Where can I follow him?

His official Instagram account is @lamineyamal and he has a TikTok account @lamine.yamal. Beware of fake accounts impersonating him.
